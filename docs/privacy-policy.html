Privacy Policy

TickTack  ·  Last updated: April 20, 2026  ·  Developer: mWn

In short: TickTack stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with third parties or our servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

TickTack does not operate any servers or databases. We do not collect, store, or process any personal data on our end. There are no analytics, tracking SDKs, or advertising frameworks in the app.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is created and stored exclusively on your device:

  • Reminders, tasks, and subtasks you create
  • Lists, tags, priorities, and repeat settings
  • Custom background photos you choose from your photo library
  • App preferences (theme, accent color, notifications settings)
  • Exported JSON backup files saved to your chosen location

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it yourself.

3. Location Data

TickTack may request access to your device's location to power location-based reminders — reminders that trigger when you arrive at or depart from a place you specify.

  • Location data is used solely to evaluate geofence conditions on your device.
  • Location coordinates are stored locally as part of your reminder data.
  • We never transmit location data to any server or third party.
  • Background location access (iOS "Always Allow" / Android background permission) is requested only to ensure geofence reminders fire when the app is not in the foreground.

You can revoke location permissions at any time in your device Settings without affecting non-location reminders.

4. Notifications

TickTack uses local push notifications to deliver your reminders. Notifications are scheduled entirely on-device using the system notification APIs (iOS: UserNotifications / Notifee; Android: Notifee). No notification content is sent to external servers.

5. Biometric Data (Face ID / Touch ID)

TickTack can lock the app with Face ID or Touch ID to protect your reminders. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by the operating system (iOS LocalAuthentication / Android BiometricPrompt). TickTack never accesses, stores, or transmits your biometric data.

6. Photo Library Access

TickTack may request access to your photo library solely to let you set a custom background image. The selected image is copied to the app's local storage and is not uploaded anywhere.

7. Data Export & Import

The export feature saves your reminders as a JSON file to a location you choose (e.g., Files app, iCloud Drive, local storage). TickTack does not control where you store this file after export. The import feature reads a previously exported JSON file — no data is sent to our servers during this process.

8. Children's Privacy

TickTack does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app collects no personal data from any user, regardless of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, the updated version will be available within the app and on our App Store / Google Play listing. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.
